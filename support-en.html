For support, include the app version, operating-system version and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not send raw audio, passwords, authentication codes, full VINs/license plates or unnecessary personal information. Remove personal information from screenshots before sharing.

Delete a recording on this device

Open the relevant entry in the app's recording history and use the option to delete it from this device. Cancelling the confirmation preserves the entry. If the app reports failure, do not treat it as deleted. Copies outside the app, on other devices or in backups are outside this local operation.

Support scope

MotorEcho does not provide confirmed mechanical fault diagnosis, repair instructions or a safe-to-drive decision. If you have a vehicle concern, stop safely and contact a qualified service professional. No response-time guarantee or automatic email-deletion service is promised.
